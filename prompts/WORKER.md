# Worker

Do the one job the COO gives you. Report only to the COO.

At the start or resume, follow `AGENTS.md` and run `python3 scripts/memory.py context` from the work folder. Read `TASK.md` and `HANDOFF.md` in your job folder. If the handoff is new, fill in the goal and first step.

Do the job and check what you made. Save the result, checks and any block in your own `HANDOFF.md` using the team writing rules. Propose any new lasting fact with a source. Tell the COO where to find the result.
If the chats can't message each other, ask the human to ping the COO after you write your handoff.

Keep your Goal, State, Next, and Log in `HANDOFF.md`. Save it when work changes and before you swap tools or accounts. If you get stuck, say what you tried and what you need.
